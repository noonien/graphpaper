# Graph Paper

A small web tool for designing and printing graph paper. Pick a pattern, adjust it, and print. Everything runs in your browser: there's nothing to install and no account.

## Patterns

- Square grid and dot grid
- Isometric lines and isometric dots
- Hexagonal
- Polar (rings and rays)
- Logarithmic and semi-log
- Ruled lines, with an optional margin line
- Music staff

## Features

- **Paper:** A5, A4, A3, Letter, Legal, Tabloid or a custom size, in portrait or landscape, with margins set for all sides or each side separately. Work in millimetres or inches.
- **Clean edges:** fit the grid to whole cells or whole major cells and center it on the page, so every edge lands on a line.
- **Major lines:** a heavier line every N cells.
- **Labels:** center axes, numbers along the edges (counted from the center or a corner), and angle and radius numbers on polar paper. Labels sit inside the grid, beside their lines, so they need no extra margin.
- **Description:** print a line on any side saying what the page is (pattern, spacing, cell count), plus your own text such as a name or date.
- **Notes area:** a ruled section below a square or dot grid.
- **Colors:** eight preset color themes, or pick your own colors and line weights.
- **Presets:** built-in setups (engineering, coordinate plane, college ruled, polar, log-log and more). Save your own setups, and share any setup as a link.
- **Printer calibration:** if your printer prints slightly too small or too large, enter the scale or measure a run of boxes on a printout. The grid is then corrected so it prints at the exact size.
- **Zoomable preview,** and SVG download for exact vector output.

## Printing tips

In your browser's print dialog:

- Set **Scale** to **100%** (not "Fit to page").
- Set **Margins** to **None** or **Default**.

To get a PDF, choose **Save as PDF** as the printer.

## Running it locally

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. It works offline.
